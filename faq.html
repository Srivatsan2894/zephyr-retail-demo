Help Center

How can we help?

Answers to the questions our customers ask most — returns, refunds, order tracking, billing, and Zephyr Rewards.

Returns & Refunds

What is Zephyr Retail's return window?

You have 30 days from the date your order is delivered to start a return. Zephyr Elite loyalty members get an extended 60-day window, and Zephyr Luxe orders get 45 days.

Does the item need to be unopened or unused to return it?

Items should be in original, unused condition with all tags, packaging, and accessories included. Items that have been worn or altered beyond trying them on may not qualify for a full refund.

How do I start a return?

Sign in, go to Order History, select the item, choose a return reason, and submit. You'll get return instructions and a prepaid shipping label by email if one is needed.

How long does a refund take, and where is it credited?

Refunds are typically processed within 3–5 business days of your return being received, plus 2–10 business days for your bank to post the funds — so most refunds appear within 5–14 business days total, credited to your original payment method.

What should I do if my refund is delayed?

First check your return status in Order History and confirm the 5–14 business day window has passed. If it has with no confirmation email, contact Support with your order number.

What if I received a damaged or incorrect item?

Start a return and select "item arrived damaged" or "received incorrect item." These cases are prioritized and may be refunded or replaced without needing to ship the item back.

Order Tracking & Delivery

Where is my order?

Track it anytime from Order History in your account, or via the link in your shipping confirmation email — it shows live status and current carrier location. You can also use our order tracking page.

How long does shipping take?

Standard delivery takes 3–7 business days, expedited takes 1–3 business days, and international delivery takes 7–14 business days depending on destination.

Why hasn't my tracking updated?

Tracking can take 24–48 hours to first appear, and carriers don't scan at every stop. If there's been no update for more than 5 business days, contact Support.

My order says "Delivered" but I don't have it — what do I do?

Check with neighbors or your mailroom, confirm your address, and wait 24 hours — carriers sometimes mark packages delivered early. Still missing after that? Contact Support with your order number.

Can I cancel or change the address on my order?

Both are possible for free as long as the order hasn't shipped yet — go to Order History and select the option. Once shipped, contact Support to see if a carrier redirect is possible.

Payment & Billing

What payment methods does Zephyr Retail accept?

Major credit and debit cards, PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and Zephyr Retail Gift Cards.

When will my card be charged?

Your card is authorized at checkout but only charged once your order ships. Multi-shipment orders are billed per shipment.

Why isn't my promo code working?

Check that the code hasn't expired, your order meets the minimum spend, and your items qualify — some promotions exclude sale items or certain categories.

Why was I charged sales tax?

Sales tax or VAT is calculated based on your shipping address per local tax law, and shown as a separate line item at checkout.

Zephyr Rewards (VIP & Loyalty)

How do I reach a higher tier like Platinum or Zephyr Elite?

Tiers are based on your rolling 12-month spend: $500+ for Gold, $1,500+ for Platinum. Zephyr Elite is reserved for roughly the top 5% of spenders and applies automatically once you qualify.

What is the Zephyr Elite "White Glove" experience?

Our top loyalty tier: priority routing to a dedicated support team, a tighter response commitment, complimentary express shipping, a 60-day return window, and a named point of contact for recurring needs.

Do my Zephyr Rewards points expire?

No, as long as you make at least one purchase every 12 months.

Zephyr Luxe

Is Zephyr Luxe support the same as Zephyr Retail support?

No — Zephyr Luxe has its own dedicated support team, separate from general Zephyr Retail agents, reachable through the Luxe site's chat, a dedicated email, and a priority phone line.

How long do I have to return a Zephyr Luxe item?

45 days from delivery — longer than the standard 30-day window — provided the item is unused with original packaging and authentication cards.

How is my Zephyr Luxe order shipped?

In branded protective packaging via insured, signature-required courier at no extra cost, with proactive tracking updates from a Luxe support agent.
